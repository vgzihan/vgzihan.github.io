Where is data saved?

Tasks are stored in your browser's local storage on this device. They are not synced online. Clearing browser site data may delete them.

Your data matters

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

In short: This static demo stores tasks in your browser's local storage. It has no built-in account system, analytics, advertising, or server-side task database.

Information stored on your device

Task titles, descriptions, and statuses are stored in local storage so they can remain available in the same browser. They are not automatically synced between devices.

Hosting providers

This project does not send task details to a FlowBoard backend. A hosting provider, such as GitHub Pages, may process technical request information under its own policies.

Cookies and tracking

This project does not intentionally use tracking or advertising cookies and includes no analytics scripts. Local storage is used for dashboard functionality.

Your choices

You can clear this site's stored data in your browser settings. This may remove saved tasks. Do not store passwords, financial details, health information, or other sensitive information here.

Changes and contact

Update this policy if you add accounts, analytics, cloud storage, or third-party services. Add verified contact details before using the site commercially.
